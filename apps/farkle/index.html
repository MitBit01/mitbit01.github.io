Farkle

Farkle is a push-your-luck dice game. The objective is to reach at least 5000 points, then have the highest score. On your turn, you roll 6 dice, aiming to roll any scoring combination. Any ⚀ or ⚄ scores alone, so look out for them! After your roll, you must set aside at least 1 scoring combination. If you cannot, you lose the points earned this turn and your turn ends. If you can, you can choose to roll again or to bank your score and end your turn. You cannot bank your score until you have at least 500 points. If you manage to score all 6 dice, you get to roll again with all 6 dice again!

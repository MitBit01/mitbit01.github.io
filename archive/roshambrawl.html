Ro-Sham-Brawl 👑⛰️ 🪨📃✂️

Multiplayer King-of-the-Hill Rock-Paper-Scissors.

How to Play

  1. Decide on a Room ID with your friends.
  2. Enter a Nickname and Room ID and click Join.
  3. Once everyone has joined, click Ready.
  4. In each Round, choose a Weapon. Rock loses to Paper, which loses to Scissors, which loses to Rock.
  5. The players who defeat the most opponents advance to the next round.
  6. The last player remaining wins!

The game currently doesn't have any cheat-protection. Only play with your friends.
